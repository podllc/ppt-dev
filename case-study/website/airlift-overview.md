# Airlift

## Technology transformation that actually works

Most organizations know their technology is holding them back. Outdated stacks. Slow delivery. Poor user experience. Rising costs. Sound familiar?

**Airlift is our prescriptive approach to technology transformation.** We embed with your teams or tackle discrete projects, bringing hands-on technical leadership that ships production-ready software while teaching your teams modern practices.

No buzzwords. No six-month consulting reports gathering dust. Just working software, delivered in 8-week cycles, that transforms how your organization builds technology.

## Two ways to work with us

### Embedded Airlift

We become part of your team for 6+ months. Think of us as the senior technical leadership you wish you had hired two years ago—shipping code, mentoring your team, and leading by example.

**Ideal for:**
- Organizations with large investments in legacy technology
- Teams struggling to adopt modern development practices
- Companies that need transformation, not just advice

**Typical engagement:**
- 1-2 senior technologists (or more, depending on need)
- Optionally includes UX leadership
- Requires executive buy-in and team acceptance

We don't consult from the sidelines. We write code. We deploy applications. We make pull requests your team reviews. Change happens when people see it work, not when they read about it in a deck.

---

### Project-Based Airlift

You have a discrete business need with defined scope and timeline. We design, build, and deploy it using modern technologies and best practices—then show your team how to replicate the approach.

**Ideal for:**
- Discrete projects with clear business goals
- Proving modern approaches before broader adoption
- Building reusable patterns for internal teams

**Typical engagement:**
- 3-6 months across three projects
- 1-2 UX leaders and 2-3 senior technologists
- Broken into phases for early incremental value

**The three-project progression:**

**Project 1:** We build it ourselves while you observe
- Create templates, patterns, and practices
- Ship production-ready software
- Document our approach

**Project 2:** We build it with your team
- Your team works alongside us
- Hands-on knowledge transfer
- You contribute code, we review and guide

**Project 3:** Your team builds it with our review
- You lead, we support
- Code reviews and architecture guidance
- Ensure quality and consistency

After three projects, you own the practices, the patterns, and the capability. No vendor dependency.

## What we transform

### The Four Disciplines

Every Airlift engagement focuses on one or more of these areas:

**1. UI & UX**
Inconsistent applications. Developers designing interfaces. Low user satisfaction. We create design systems, pattern libraries, and user experiences that actually work—and teach your team to maintain them.

**2. DevOps**
No CI/CD. Manual deployments. Cloud infrastructure that nobody understands. We build automated pipelines, infrastructure as code, and deployment strategies that let you ship confidently.

**3. Process & Product**
Ad-hoc planning. No visibility into delivery. Stakeholders asking "when will it be done?" every week. We implement practical Agile processes (not dogmatic nonsense) that give teams focus and leaders transparency.

**4. Architecture**
Technology decisions made five years ago that haunt you today. We assess your platform, design for the future, and make pragmatic technology choices that serve your business—not our resumes.

## How it works

**Assessment (1-2 weeks)**

We assess your current situation using maturity models across the four disciplines. You get a diagnostic, a cost/risk assessment, and a defined plan with 1-3 identified projects.

We involve your team: stakeholder interviews, workshops, self-assessments. Transformation requires buy-in, and buy-in requires understanding where you are and where you're going.

**Delivery (8-week business cycles)**

Once we know what needs to change, we deliver in 8-week cycles. Each cycle is transformative to the business—not "progress toward a future state," but working software that solves real problems.

- **Design**: Detailed planning across disciplines
- **Development**: Production-ready deliveries every 2 weeks
- **Support**: Knowledge transfer and ongoing assistance

**Knowledge Transfer**

We're not building dependency. We're building capability. By the time we leave, your team owns the practices, the patterns, and the tools. You're not stuck calling us every time something breaks.
